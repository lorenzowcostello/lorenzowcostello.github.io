School 2025

Sulfuric Acid Production Design Project

In this final project, three other students and I were tasked with designing a theoretical sulfuric acid production plant.

+

Overview

Using the single contact process we developed a process flow diagram, conducted mass and energy balances, and evaluted safety, economic, and environmental considerations.

My role

I independently completed the process flow diagram, mass balances and economic considerations. I also collaborated on researching the single contact process and energy balances.

Process

Since this project was quite large, our first step as a group was desginating sections to each person, and creating scheduled, in-person meetings each week to update each other on what we have done. This was helpful as not only did it allow people to work invidivually, the meetings enabled us to collaborate and help our teammates if they were struggling.

Outcome

Throughout this project, I learned the importance of establishing a strong understanding of the overall process before jumping into material and energy balances. Carefully choosing the appropriate production method and developing clear, straightforward diagrams made later calculations much simpler to understand.

School 2025

2-Phase Flash Calculation Using MATLAB

Here, I created a MATLAB script to identify and plot the behaviour of an isobutane-carbon dioxide system.

+

Overview

To achieve this goal, I looped a 2-phase flash calculation, using the Rachford-Rice and Peng-Robinson equations. The results were then plotted on a Pressure vs CO2 Mol Ratio graph.

My role

As this project was independent, I wrote both the MATLAB code and the written assignment.

Process

My first step in completing this project was fully understanding the block flow diagram, to fully understand the project's direction. Second was reviewing the PR equation to establish which parameters I would need to account for in my script. The final stage was building the code, which included a main script which built the plot, along with multiple driver functions, including a Newton-Raphson solver and a Cardano root solver.

Outcome

This project taught me how to digest a large project in several small bites, as I had to build each driver funciton before implementing them all in the main script, with my final grade being a 100. Furthermore, it solidifed my proficiency in MATLAB and gave me a new understanding of the Peng-Robinson equation of state and its parameters.

School 2025

Investigation Into the Exxon Valdez Oil Spill Cleanup

For the final project in our Environmental Bioremediation course, myself and a team of 2 investigated the abiotic and bioremediation methods for the Exxon Valdez Oil Spill.

+

Overview

This project encompasses the oil spill itself, the cleanup methods used, the effects on the environment and people, along with our reccomendations and conclusions. The paper served as a guideline for our final presentation on the topic.

My role

In this project, I was in charge of researching and writing the abiotic and bioremediation methods used.

Process

The first thing our team did was a qualitative research into the oil spill itself - how did it happen, what were the effects, how long did it take to clean up. Next, I researched the various cleanup methods used, such as the abiotic mechanical removal or the injection of fertelizer to stimulate microorganisms.

Outcome

This project, and course in general, really shifted my focus onto environmental challenges that come with modern day technology. After taking this course in Fall of 2025, I decided to pursue a minor in environmental engineering.

Research 2026

Undergraduate Research

Over Summer 2026, I worked under a PhD student to research the co-electrosynthesis of hydroxylamine and FDCA in a bipolar membrane electrode assembly.

+

Overview

I decided to join Dr. Ali Seifitokaldani's lab and this project specifically because of the enviornmental applications of this research. Electrosynthesis is a much more environmentally friendly way to produce hydroxlamine and FDCA than the current industrial methods, which aligns with my interest in sustainable process engineering.

My role

Over the summer, I indpendently ran electrocatalytic experiments using a flow cell, measuring voltage, and obtained results using gas chromatogrpahy. I used the data to calculate parameters like the faradaic effeciency in Excel, where I also prepared plots and bar charts. I also prepared literature reviews for potential catalysts. Lastly, I gave several 'work update' presentations, which I presented to the team throughout the summer.

Takeaways

Throughout the experience, I learned how to operate safely and effectivley in a laboratory enviornment. I also learned how to communicate complex information through diagrams and plots, furthering my proficiency with Microsoft Excel.
