School 2025

Sulfuric Acid Production Design Project

In this final project, three other students and I were tasked with designing a theoretical sulfuric acid production plant.

+

Overview

Using the single contact process we developed a process flow diagram, conducted mass and energy balances, and evaluted safety, economic, and environmental considerations.

My role

I independently completed the process flow diagram, mass balances and economic considerations. I also collaborated on researching the single contact process and energy balances.

Process

The first step was evaulating and choosing the single contact process, due to its high conversion rate of sulfur dioxide. Next was creating the process flow diagram, helping to lay out the foundations for the bulk of the work. After which come mass balances, and energy balances, using the heat of formation method. After the balances were conducted, safety, economic and eviornmental consdierations were completed.

Outcome

Throughout this project, I learned the importance of establishing a strong understanding of the overall process before jumping into material and energy balances. Carefully choosing the appropriate production method and developing clear, straightforward diagrams made later calculations much simpler to understand.

School 2025

2-Phase Flash Calculation Using MATLAB

Here, I created a MATLAB script to identify and plot the behaviour of an isobutane-carbon dioxide system.

+

Overview

To achieve this goal, I looped a 2-phase flash calculation, using the Rachford-Rice and Peng-Robinson equations. The results were then plotted on a Pressure vs CO2 Mol Ratio graph.

My role

As this project was independent, I wrote both the MATLAB code and the written assignment.

Process

My first step in completing this project was fully understanding the block flow diagram, to fully understand the project's direction. Second was reviewing the PR equation to establish which parameters I would need to account for in my script. The final stage was building the code, which included a main script which built the plot, along with multiple driver functions, including a Newton-Raphson solver and a Cardano root solver.

Outcome

This project taught me how to digest a large project in several small bites, as I had to build each driver funciton before implementing them all in the main script, with my final grade being a 100. Furthermore, it solidifed my proficiency in MATLAB and gave me a new understanding of the Peng-Robinson equation of state and its parameters.
